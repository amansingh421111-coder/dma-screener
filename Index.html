How to read this screen

The % under the price is the distance from the stock's moving average. "−0.17% vs 44-DMA" means today's price is 0.17% below the average of its last 44 closing prices.

Signals. Buy: price just crossed above the average. Sell: it just crossed below. Approaching: within 1.5% of the average, a possible cross soon.

The bar: the centre line is the average; the dot shows where price sits from −5% to +5%; the shaded zone is the "approaching" band.

Stars (0-3): one each for a candle that agrees with the expected move, a close near the day's high (buys) or low (sells), and volume at least 1.5x its 20-day average. RSI above 70 is stretched, below 30 is oversold. 200-DMA shows the long-term trend. Days is how long price has been on its current side of the average.
