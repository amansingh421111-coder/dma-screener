How to read this screen

vs 44-DMA is how far the price is from its 44-day moving average. "−0.17%" means 0.17% below the average of the last 44 closes.

Two views. Crossed today = stocks whose price crossed the average on the latest trading day (buy: crossed above, sell: crossed below). In range = stocks sitting near the average whether or not they crossed today. Signals. Buy: price is at or above the average, up to 5% above it. Approaching: price is below the average but within 2% of it. Sell: price just crossed below the average.

Stars (0-3): the candle agrees with the expected move, it closed near the day's high (buys) or low (sells), and volume is at least 1.5x its 20-day average. Market cap is in rupees crore (Large, Mid, Small, Micro are approximate buckets). RSI above 70 is stretched, below 30 is oversold. Days is how long price has been on its current side of the average.
