How to read this screen

Screening: Buy means the latest close crossed above the 44-DMA; Sell means it crossed below; Approaching means it is within the configured band. The screener also shows candle, volume, RSI, 200-DMA and 52-week context.

Positions: Add a purchased stock with quantity, entry, stop and target. The tracker gives a rule-based Hold / Watch / Stop Loss / Target indication. It is a decision-support tool, not financial advice.
